How Do You Earn Points?

+1

Every Valid Sample

Earn one point for each valid image-caption sample.

+5

Milestone Bonus

Receive five bonus points for every 50 samples submitted.

+2

Daily Streak

Submit at least five samples daily for three consecutive days. Awarded once per qualifying streak.

+10

Weekly Warrior

Earn ten bonus points when you contribute more than 50 samples in one week.
